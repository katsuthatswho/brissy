Schedule reality check

You're arriving Sunday 11 October around 8:30pm and leaving Sunday 18 around 11am. The PPA runs 13–18 October, so Monday is free and you will miss Sunday's finals. Match schedules aren't confirmed here; check the official order of play before locking in each afternoon.

Brisbane is one hour behind Sydney. If you work Sydney hours, 9am–5pm Sydney means 8am–4pm Brisbane. Afternoon PPA visits therefore require finishing work early or taking leave.
