Easiest: rideshare

Uber or DiDi from the airport rideshare pickup zone to your accommodation. Compare live quotes; late arrival and luggage make this the low-stress option.

Cheaper alternative: Airtrain

Airtrain to South Brisbane, then a short Uber/DiDi or local bus to West End. Airport trains operate until about 10pm, but check your terminal's last departure; the airport leg is NOT 50c.

If you're hungry

Antep Chargrill & Kebab on Russell St lists Sunday service until 11pm. Intermission, 125 Boundary St, lists Sunday until midnight but is more a bar/snacks option. Call/check kitchen hours before travelling.

If you just need supplies

Harris Farm on Boundary St lists 10pm closing. Petra 1 on Boundary St lists midnight; A1 Mart on Vulture St lists midnight; 7-Eleven at 80 Vulture St lists 24 hours.

Brisbane vs Sydney · useful quirks

Clock change

Brisbane is one hour behind Sydney during NSW daylight saving. Sydney 9am = Brisbane 8am in October.

No daylight saving

Queensland stays on AEST (UTC+10) all year; Sydney moves to AEDT (UTC+11) in October.

Sunday shopping

Large West End supermarkets typically close at 6pm Sunday, earlier than weekdays. Do not assume Sydney-style late supermarket hours.

50c public transport

Most local Translink journeys are only 50c. Ferry rides can be a very cheap way to see the river. Airport Airtrain is the major exception.

Coffee culture

Specialty coffee bars often start early and close mid-afternoon; check filter availability and laptop policies. Some great coffee spots are designed for quick visits, not a full workday.

Humidity and storms

October can feel warmer and more humid than Sydney. Pack water, sunscreen and a light rain layer; check the forecast before river walks.

Groceries, convenience and everyday life

Best produce stop

Harris Farm Markets, 97 Boundary St. Listed daily 7am–10pm, a useful option for a late-ish grocery run.

Main supermarkets

Coles at Boundary/Mollison and Woolworths at West Village: listed weekdays 7am–9pm, Sunday 9am–6pm. Woolworths Montague Markets and ALDI are other options depending on where you stay.

After-hours fallback

7-Eleven, 80 Vulture St (24 hours); Petra 1, 141 Boundary St (listed until midnight Sunday); A1 Mart, 60 Vulture St (listed until midnight Sunday).

Asian groceries

Sunlit Asian Supermarket, 69–79 Boundary St; Westie Market, 258 Montague Rd. Useful for snacks and quick meals, but check Sunday closing.

Food delivery

Uber Eats and DoorDash are options if you arrive too late or don't want to venture out. Check what is actually accepting orders rather than relying on listed shop hours.

Morning, nightlife and closing times

Morning

Brisbane is an early coffee city. Plan destination coffee stops in the morning, not at 4pm. Many brunch kitchens close before dinner.

West End at night

Boundary Street is a practical first stop for casual bars and food. West End isn't uniformly late-night: kitchens may close before bars.

Going out-out

Fortitude Valley is Brisbane's main late-night entertainment district; West End is generally more neighbourhood bars, music and casual dining.

Friday/Saturday transport

Nightlink late-night buses operate Friday and Saturday nights, generally midnight–5am, with routes from the city and Fortitude Valley. Don't count on the same service on Sunday night.

Dinner strategy

For destination restaurants, book ahead and check kitchen last orders. Sunday and Monday closures are common enough to verify before travelling.
